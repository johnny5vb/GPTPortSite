# The weekly review is the only load-bearing habit

> [!abstract] The claim
> Every other part of a second brain is optional scaffolding; without a weekly
> pass through the inbox, the whole thing degrades into a junk drawer.

## Why it holds

*(Seed — this note is deliberately unfinished so you can see what a seed looks
like on the Dashboard. Develop it, then flip `status` to `permanent`.)*

Rough argument: capture is only valuable if something later distinguishes signal
from noise. The review is that filter. Plugins, templates, and folder schemes
change what capture *feels* like; only the review changes what the vault
*contains*.
