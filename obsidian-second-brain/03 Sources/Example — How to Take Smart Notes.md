# Example — How to Take Smart Notes

## In one line
Writing isn't what you do *after* thinking; the note-taking is the thinking.

## Highlights
- Paraphrase in your own words at capture time — the translation is where
  comprehension actually happens.
- A note you can't connect to anything is a note you've already lost.
