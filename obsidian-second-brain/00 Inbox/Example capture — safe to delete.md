# Example capture — safe to delete

This is what a raw capture looks like: no structure, no tags, no decision made.
It sits here until the weekly review, where exactly one of three things happens
to it.

- **Delete it** — most captures. This is the correct outcome and it is not a failure.
- **File it** — move to `02 Projects`, `03 Sources`, or `05 People` as-is.
- **Promote it** — rewrite as an atomic note in `01 Notes` with a claim for a title.

Delete this note once you've read it. Then check the Dashboard — the Inbox
table should be empty, which is the state you want it in every Monday morning.
